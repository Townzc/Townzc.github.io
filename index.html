2026Under Review · AAAI

Affinity-Guided Filtering for Image Matting without Alpha Labels

Research Collaborator

An alpha-label-free semi-supervised matting framework that refines model predictions with an Affinity-Guided Filtering Function and reuses them as pseudo-labels. The approach recovers fine transition details on AM-2K, P3M-10K, and LIDC without additional inference-time computation.

2026Accepted · ICONIP 2026

GASP-SAM: Lightweight Geometry-Aware Semantic Prompting for Abdominal Organ Segmentation

Project Leader

A parameter-efficient SAM ViT-B adaptation combining LoRA, BioMedCLIP semantic prompting, geometry-guided residual fusion, and local boundary refinement. It achieved 90.86% mean Dice on FLARE22 and 87.82% on Synapse with 8.10M trainable parameters.
