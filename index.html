GASP-SAM: Lightweight Geometry-Aware Semantic Prompting for Abdominal Organ Segmentation

Project Leader

A two-stage SAM adaptation framework for box-prompted abdominal multi-organ CT segmentation, designed to reduce domain shift, add organ-specific semantics to spatial prompts, and correct local boundary errors without disrupting reliable coarse predictions.

  • Adapted SAM ViT-B with LoRA while keeping the SAM prompt encoder and BioMedCLIP text encoder frozen, reaching strong medical-domain transfer with only 8.10M trainable parameters.
  • Designed organ-aware semantic prompt learning and TextCIM cross-modal interaction to inject anatomical identity into dense SAM image features.
  • Introduced geometry-guided residual fusion with boundary, center, and signed-distance cues so structural priors remain active during inference.
  • Built a compact local residual refiner that corrects boundary errors only within ROI regions, avoiding unnecessary modification of reliable coarse predictions.
  • Implemented the full PyTorch training and evaluation pipeline, including LoRA adaptation, semantic-geometry supervision, staged training, and 3D reconstruction-based evaluation.
